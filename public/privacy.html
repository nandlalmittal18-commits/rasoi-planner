Privacy Policy

Rasoi Planner · Effective 13 November 2026

The short version

  • If you don’t sign in, your family details and menus stay on your phone. Nothing is sent to us.
  • If you sign in with Google, we save your family details and menus so they appear on every phone you use.
  • We use your data only to plan and show your menus. We don’t sell it, show ads or share it for marketing.
  • You can delete your data at any time with Family → Start over, or by emailing us.

Who we are

Rasoi Planner is an app that plans weekly breakfast, lunch and dinner menus for households in India. In this policy, “we” means the Rasoi Planner team. You can reach us at nandlalmittal18@gmail.com.

What we collect

Data When Why
Your name, email address, profile picture link and Google account ID When you choose “Continue with Google” To sign you in and keep your family’s data in your account
Family settings: family name, how many adults and children eat at home, Jain or no onion-garlic, weekly fasting days and the festivals you keep, favourite cuisines, tiffin, cooking time, your cook’s first name, dishes you like or don’t want When you fill in the Family screen To plan menus that follow your family’s food rules
Your cook’s WhatsApp number, only if you turn on “Daily WhatsApp to the cook” and confirm the cook agreed When you fill it in on the Family screen To send your cook tomorrow’s menu each evening. Turn it off or delete the number at any time; the cook can also reply STOP
Your weekly menu and the shopping items you tick As you use the app To show the same menu and list on all your devices

Some family settings, such as fasting days or Jain food, can say something about your religious practice. We treat them as sensitive and use them only to plan your menus.

We do not collect your own phone number, location, contacts, payment details or health records, and we do not collect any details about individual children (only how many there are).

When you don’t sign in

Everything is stored only in your phone’s web browser. We never receive it. Clearing your browser data or using “Start over” removes it.

Who else handles your data

We use a few trusted services to run the app. They process data only to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase stores your account and family data in a secure database and runs the sign-in.
  • Google confirms who you are when you sign in. We receive only your name, email and profile picture link.
  • Vercel hosts the app. Like any website host, it briefly records technical details such as your IP address.
  • Meta (WhatsApp Business) delivers the daily menu message to your cook, if you turn it on. It receives the cook’s number, first name and the menu text.

When you tap a WhatsApp button, the message is created on your phone and sent through WhatsApp. WhatsApp’s own privacy policy applies to it, and we cannot see your chats.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers.

How we protect it

  • All connections use encryption (HTTPS).
  • Database rules make sure each signed-in account can read and change only its own family’s data.
  • Only the Rasoi Planner team can access the database, and only to run and support the app.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while you use your account. Family → Start over deletes your family settings, menu and shopping list from our database straight away. To delete your sign-in account as well, email us and we will delete it within 30 days. Signing out removes the copy kept on that phone.

Your rights

Under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can:

  • ask what data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • ask us to correct or complete it (you can also edit it yourself on the Family screen);
  • ask us to delete it, or withdraw your consent by signing out and asking us to delete your account;
  • nominate someone to use these rights for you if you are unable to;
  • raise a complaint with us. If we don’t resolve it, you can approach the Data Protection Board of India.

Email nandlalmittal18@gmail.com with your request. We aim to reply within 7 days and to complete requests within 30 days.

Age

Rasoi Planner is meant for adults who plan meals for their household. Please don’t sign in if you are under 18.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If a change affects how we use your data, we will tell you in the app before it applies.
